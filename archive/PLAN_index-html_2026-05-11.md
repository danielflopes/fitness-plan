## Training — 4-Day Split

**Format:** Each session, pick 4 exercises · 3 sets each · near failure · ~45 min.
**Progressive overload:** All reps clean → +2.5kg next session. Fail a rep → hold weight until clean.
**Rest:** 2–3 min compound · 60–90s isolation.

### Achilles — Alfredson Eccentric Protocol (callout in Training tab)

> Evidence-based. Takes 6–12 weeks. Consistency is everything.
> 3×15 eccentric calf raises on a step, twice daily. Rise on both feet. Lower slowly on the affected leg only over 3 seconds. Discomfort ≤5/10 acceptable. Stop if sharp pain.

**Press cue:** Push upper back into bench; let shoulders come slightly forward at lockout instead of pinning blades hard.

## Cardio — Return-to-Run Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Weeks 1–4 (Achilles First. No running while symptomatic.)
- Brisk walking, outdoors, 1–2×/week, 30 min.
- Stationary bike / cycling, Zone 2, 20–30 min.
- Eccentric calf raises: 3×15 (the actual treatment).

### Phase 2 — Weeks 5–8 (Return to Run. Only if Achilles pain-free for 1 week.)
- Week 5–6: Walk 3min / Jog 1min × 5 rounds (20 min). 1×/week.
- Week 7: Walk 2min / Jog 2min × 5 rounds (20 min). 1×/week.
- Week 8: Walk 1min / Jog 3min × 5 rounds (20 min). 2×/week.

### Phase 3 — Weeks 9–12 (Back to your level and beyond.)
- Week 9–10: 20 min continuous run at 6:00/km. 1×/week.
- Week 11: 25 min run, try 5:45/km. 1×/week.
- Week 12: 30 min run at comfortable pace. 1×/week.

### Zone 2 Principle
Keep 80% of cardio in Zone 2 — conversational pace, HR ~120–140 bpm.

## Habits

### Sleep — #1 Performance Variable
- No screens 30 min before bed (or night mode aggressively).
- When sleep is bad — skip gym, walk + mobility only.

### Daily Movement
- 20-min walk at end of day, every day including rest.
- Stand up every 45–60 min.
- 8,000+ steps/day target.

### Training Consistency System
- Minimum viable workout rule: tired? Do 2 exercises. Never skip entirely.
- Never let 2 become 3: 1 missed = nothing. 2 = refocus now.

### 3-Month Progress Milestones
- **Month 1:** Mobility habit locked in · split feels natural · sleep routine · Achilles improving.
- **Month 2:** Visible strength gains · body comp shifting · running return begins.
- **Month 3:** BF heading toward 18–19% · clear muscle definition · running 25–30 min pain-free.
