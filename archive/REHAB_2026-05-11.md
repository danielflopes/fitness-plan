## Phase 1: Isometric Loading (Weeks 1–4)

**Goal:** Pain reduction, tendon load tolerance

| Exercise | Prescription | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Isometric calf hold (wall) | 5 × 45s | Daily |
| Eccentric calf raises (Alfredson) | 3 × 15 (straight + bent knee) | Daily |
| Seated calf raise isometric | 3 × 45s | Daily |

**Running:** None. No jumping, no skipping.
**Cardio:** Bike only, Zone 2, ≤30min.
**Stop rule:** Pain >3/10 during exercise → reduce load or volume.

---

## Phase 2: Heavy Slow Resistance (Weeks 5–8)

**Goal:** Tendon remodeling under load

| Exercise | Prescription | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Heavy slow calf raise (3s up, 3s down) | 3 × 8 @ tolerable load | 3×/week |
| Single-leg calf raise (eccentric bias) | 3 × 10 | 3×/week |
| Soleus bridge | 3 × 12 | 3×/week |

**Running:** Walk-jog intervals. Start 1min jog / 2min walk × 6. Progress to 3min jog / 1min walk.
**Stop rule:** Pain >3/10 during or >24h next-day soreness → drop back to Phase 1 protocol.

---

## Phase 3: Return to Running (Weeks 9–12)

**Goal:** Sustained pain-free running at 4km

| Exercise | Prescription | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance calf raises | 3 × 12 moderate load | 2×/week |
| Single-leg hop test | Pass: 10 consecutive pain-free | Weekly check |
| Calf raise endurance | Target: 25+ reps affected side | Weekly check |

**Running:** 2× per week, 4km easy (Zone 2, conversational pace).
**Stop rule:** Pain >3/10 during run → walk remainder, drop to Phase 2 walk-jog for 1 week.

---

## Run-Readiness Criteria

Before progressing to the next phase, all must be met:

- [ ] Pain ≤2/10 during current phase exercises for 5+ consecutive sessions
- [ ] No next-day soreness lasting >24h
- [ ] Phase-specific milestone achieved (see each phase above)

Before starting Phase 3 running:
- [ ] Single-leg hop test: 10 consecutive, pain-free
- [ ] Calf raise endurance: 25+ reps on affected side
- [ ] Walk-jog at 3:1 ratio for 25min with zero pain
