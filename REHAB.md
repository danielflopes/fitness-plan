## Phase 1: Eccentric Loading (Weeks 1–6, starting 2026-05-11)

**Goal:** Tendon load tolerance + pain reduction via daily heavy slow loading.

### Alfredson Protocol — Twice Daily (AM + PM)

Two movements, 3×15 each, 3s eccentric. **90 reps per session, 180/day.**

| Movement | Targets | Rise (concentric) | Lower (eccentric) | Added weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Straight leg | Gastrocnemius | Both feet | Both feet (wks 1–2) → one foot (wks 3+) | 5–10kg DB to start; +2.5–5kg when easy |
| Bent knee | Soleus | Both feet | Both feet (wks 1–2) → one foot (wks 3+) | Same progression |

Gate to one-foot lowering (week 3): pain ≤2/10 through week 2.
Gate to adding more load: 3×15 at current weight with pain ≤1/10.

### Phase 1 cardio

- **Bike Zone 2** only (30–45 min, Saturday + ad-hoc commute rides).
- **Walking** daily (20 min, end of day).
- **No running, no jumping, no skipping, no kettlebell swings.**

### Phase 1 stop rules

- Pain >3/10 during reps → reduce to 3×8 for both sets, hold 3 sessions, reassess.
- Pain >24h after → drop to 1× daily for 1 week.
- Sharp pain → stop, log below, evaluate before next session.

## Phase 2: Walk-Jog Reintroduction (Weeks 5–8)

**Goal:** Reintroduce running impact with progressive jog ratio.

### Cardio progression (Saturdays unless noted)

- Wk 5–6: Walk 3min / Jog 1min × 5 rounds (20 min), 1×/week.
- Wk 7: Walk 2min / Jog 2min × 5 rounds (20 min), 1×/week.
- Wk 8: Walk 1min / Jog 3min × 5 rounds (20 min), **2×/week**.

### Phase 2 loading (continue, with progression)

Drop bilateral eccentrics. Move to **unilateral eccentric + heavy slow resistance**:

| Movement | Sets × Reps | Tempo | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single-leg calf raise (eccentric bias) | 3 × 10 each side | 3s up, 3s down | Add load (DB in hand) once 3×10 BW is easy. |
| Soleus bridge / Seated heavy calf raise | 3 × 12 | 3s up, 3s down | Bent knee, load-tolerable. |

Twice daily continues if pain stays ≤2/10. Drop to 1×/day if walk-jog adds soreness.

### Phase 2 stop rules

- Pain >3/10 during or >24h after → drop back to Phase 1 walk + cycling for 1 week, then retry the prior week's interval.
- Two failed retries → reset to Phase 1 protocol, reassess in 2 weeks.

## Phase 3: Continuous Running (Weeks 9–12)

**Goal:** Sustained pain-free running at 4km, twice weekly.

### Cardio progression

- Wk 9–10: 20 min continuous run at ~6:00/km, 1×/week.
- Wk 11: 25 min at ~5:45/km, 1–2×/week.
- Wk 12: 30 min comfortable, **2×/week. 4km × 2/week target hit here.**

### Phase 3 maintenance loading

- Single-leg calf raises 3 × 12 moderate load, 2×/week (sufficient to maintain tendon adaptation).
- Single-leg hop test weekly: aim 10 consecutive pain-free both sides.

### Phase 3 stop rules

- Pain >3/10 during a run → walk remainder, drop back to Phase 2 walk-jog for 1 week.
- Hill running: **defer until at least 4 weeks pain-free at 4km flat.** Hills were the original trigger.
