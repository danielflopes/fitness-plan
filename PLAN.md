## Cardio — Block 1 (Weeks 1–4): Pure Eccentric Loading

**Saturday session:** Zone 2 bike, 30–45 min, outdoors preferred. **No running, no jump rope, no kettlebell swings.**

Why: 4-week pure eccentric block before reintroducing impact. Lets the bilateral tendon adapt before progressing.

Bad weather fallback: stationary bike or elliptical, same duration.

### Block 2 (Weeks 5–8) — Walk-Jog Reintroduction

**Gate:** Pain ≤2/10 during eccentrics for 5 consecutive sessions + 25+ single-leg calf raises both sides (see REHAB.md).

- Wk 5–6: Walk 3min / Jog 1min × 5 rounds (20 min), 1×/week.
- Wk 7: Walk 2min / Jog 2min × 5 rounds (20 min), 1×/week.
- Wk 8: Walk 1min / Jog 3min × 5 rounds (20 min), 2×/week.

Stop rule: pain >3/10 during or >24h after → drop back 1 step, hold for 1 week.

### Block 3 (Weeks 9–12) — 4km Pain-Free × 2/week (Goal)

- Wk 9–10: 20 min continuous run at ~6:00/km, 1×/week.
- Wk 11: 25 min at ~5:45/km, 1–2×/week.
- Wk 12: 30 min comfortable, 2×/week. **4km × 2/week target hit here.**

**Zone 2 principle:** 80% of cardio at conversational pace, HR ~120–140 bpm.

## Achilles Rehab — Daily

Full protocol in **`REHAB.md`**. Headline:

- **Alfredson:** 3×15 eccentric calf raises, twice daily (AM + PM), starting day 1.
- Weeks 1–2: bilateral concentric + bilateral eccentric (both feet lower together).
- Weeks 3–6: bilateral concentric + **unilateral eccentric** lowering (alternating sides).
- Pain ≤3/10 acceptable during/after. Sharp pain → stop.
