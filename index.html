Your 4-Day Split
Each session: pick 4 exercises · 3 sets each · near failure · ~45 min. Tap a day to expand.
Progressive Overload Rules
▸All reps clean → add 2.5kg next session
▸Fail a rep → keep same weight until all sets are clean
▸Rest → 2–3 min compound · 60–90s isolation
▸Log every session → even a notes app works
Evidence-based. Takes 6–12 weeks. Consistency is everything.
▸Protocol 3×15 eccentric calf raises on a step, twice daily (AM + PM).
▸How Rise on both feet. Lower slowly on affected leg only over 3 seconds.
▸Pain rule Discomfort ≤5/10 acceptable. Stop immediately if sharp.
Phase Progression
▸Wk 1–2 Bilateral concentric + bilateral eccentric — both feet lower together.
▸Wk 3–6 Bilateral concentric + unilateral eccentric — alternating sides, 3s down.
Stop Rules
▸Pain >3/10 → reduce to 3×8, hold 3 sessions, reassess.
▸Sharp pain → stop the session and log it in REHAB.md.
▸Pain >24h after → drop back one block step.
Gate to Block 2: pain ≤2/10 for 5 consecutive sessions + 25+ single-leg calf raises both sides.
