Achilles — Alfredson Eccentric Protocol
Evidence-based. Takes 6–12 weeks. Consistency is everything.
3×15 eccentric calf raises on a step, twice daily. Rise on both feet. Lower slowly on the affected leg only over 3 seconds. Discomfort ≤5/10 acceptable. Stop if sharp pain.

Key Rules
▸Protein at every meal — non-negotiable for recomp
▸More carbs on training days — less on rest days
▸Don't skip breakfast — morning training needs fuel
▸Meal prep on Sundays — prevents random eating
▸Target 2.5L water/day — 500ml on waking, 500ml during workout
Sleep — Your #1 Performance Variable
Your consistency drops when sleep is bad — so sleep is the most important habit to protect.
▸Target 7.5h — consistent bedtime (e.g. 22:45 → 06:15)
▸Same wake time every day — even weekends. Biggest lever.
▸No screens 30 min before bed — or night mode aggressively
▸Room cool & dark — ~18°C is optimal
▸When sleep is bad — skip gym, walk + mobility only
Daily Movement
▸20-min walk at end of day — every day, including rest days
▸Stand up every 45–60 min — set a timer if needed
▸Take stairs always
▸Target 8,000+ steps/day — the walk covers a large part of this
Training Consistency System
▸Plan sessions on Sunday — in your calendar like meetings
▸Minimum viable workout rule — tired? Do 2 exercises. Never skip entirely.
▸Log every session — weights used, in a notes app
▸Morning anchor — lay out clothes the night before
▸Never let 2 become 3 — 1 missed = nothing. 2 = refocus now.
