Achilles Rehab — Daily, Non-Negotiable
Every day, AM + PM where noted. Consistency is the treatment — not intensity.
Isometric calf holds — 5×45s, daily.
▸How Rise to the top of a calf raise (both feet or step edge) and hold. No movement — pure isometric tension.
▸Why first Isometrics reduce tendon pain fastest and don't add eccentric load while the tendon is most reactive.
Run alongside Phase 2 below once pain allows — they're not strictly sequential.
3×15 eccentric calf raises on a step, twice daily (AM + PM). Takes 6–12 weeks — consistency is everything.
▸How Rise on both feet. Lower slowly on affected leg only over 3 seconds.
Progression
▸Wk 1–2 Bilateral concentric + bilateral eccentric — both feet lower together.
▸Wk 3–6 Bilateral concentric + unilateral eccentric — alternating sides, 3s down.
Gate to Phase 3: pain ≤2/10 for 5 consecutive sessions + 25+ single-leg calf raises both sides.
Heavy slow resistance (HSR) calf raises — 3×8 at a tolerable load, once Phase 2 is cleared.
▸How Weighted (barbell, DB, or plate on lap seated) calf raise, 3s up + 3s down, full range. Add load week to week as pain allows.
▸Why Builds tendon capacity for real load — the step between rehab reps and running.
Only once running is pain-free. One at a time — reactive loading is a stressor.
